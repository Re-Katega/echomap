DATA NOTICE

NO ACCOUNT OR IDENTITY DETAILS ARE REQUESTED BY ECHOMAP.

EchoMap has no user account system or search-history database. News searches use a small Netlify Function.

Your place and optional keyword are sent through EchoMap's Netlify Function to Bing News; clicked map coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to identify the place. If you choose to open a fallback search link, Google News receives that search. The app keeps results temporarily in the open page. Netlify and the providers may process connection data under their own policies. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

PURPOSE & LIMITS

Public-event research, not private-person investigation.

  • Designed to use publicly available information and authorized APIs.
  • Not designed to access private accounts, messages, passwords, restricted databases, or information requiring unauthorized access.
  • Focused on publicly reported events; avoid investigating or targeting private individuals.
  • Original publisher links are shown when the feed provides them.
  • Public reports can be wrong, outdated, incomplete, or conflicting. Verify claims with original sources.
  • Use the app and each external service in accordance with applicable laws and their terms.

Privacy Policy

This draft describes the current EchoMap web app, its Netlify Function, and its third-party requests. It is not a guarantee of legal compliance.

Before public launch: add the project operator's legal name, hosting provider, and applicable jurisdiction. For privacy questions, contact echomapwebsite@gmail.com. Legal requirements depend on where the service is operated and used.

1. Who operates EchoMap

The operator's legal identity has not been provided. For privacy questions or requests, contact echomapwebsite@gmail.com. The app has no account service or search-history database. A small Netlify Function forwards news searches to Bing News.

2. Information processed to provide the app

  • News search: the place text, optional keyword, English-language market, and (for recent searches) a coarse 7-, 30-, or 365-day freshness window are sent from your browser to an EchoMap Netlify Function, which forwards the query to Bing News RSS. Bing receives the search query; Netlify may process the request and connection data. The exact selected date range is applied in your browser.
  • Optional fallback search: if Bing returns no matches or is unavailable, the app may offer a Google News search link. Only if you open that link does your browser send the place, optional keyword, and dates to Google News.
  • Map lookup: when you click the map, the clicked latitude, longitude, and map zoom are sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to resolve a place name.
  • Map tiles: the browser requests visible map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Normal connection information is sent as part of those requests.
  • External assets: the app loads the Share Tech Mono font from Google Fonts and Leaflet files from unpkg. Those services receive browser requests for the assets.
  • Browser memory: search results are held in page memory while the page is open. The app may reuse a matching result for 60 seconds. The app does not write searches to cookies, local storage, or a search-history database.

3. Why these requests are made

Search text is used to request matching news; map coordinates are used to identify the selected place; map tile and asset requests are used to display the interface. Do not enter sensitive information, passwords, private-person details, or information you are not authorized to share.

4. Third-party services and hosting

News searches pass through an EchoMap Netlify Function before being forwarded to Bing News. Netlify may receive the search query and technical data such as an IP address, browser information, request time, and requested URL. Map, map-tile, font, and library requests go directly from your browser to their providers. The app does not intentionally store search requests or maintain server-side search history; Netlify may process request data under its own terms and policies. EchoMap does not control provider systems, retention, or practices. Review the current notices for Netlify, Microsoft/Bing, OpenStreetMap Foundation, and Google.

5. Storage, retention, and security

Search results and the short-lived cache stay in browser memory and the app does not maintain a server-side search history. Memory is normally cleared when the page is closed or reloaded; a cached result may be reused for 60 seconds while the page remains open. Netlify and other providers may process request data under their own retention practices. Internet transmission and third-party services cannot be guaranteed to be risk-free.

6. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to use search, avoid entering optional keywords, block external requests in your browser, or close the page. Blocking requests can prevent maps, fonts, or reports from working. For questions or requests, contact echomapwebsite@gmail.com.

7. Changes and legal review

This policy should be reviewed whenever providers, hosting, data flows, or features change. The operator should obtain jurisdiction-specific legal advice and update this notice before public deployment.

METHODOLOGY

How EchoMap searches

  1. Resolve a clicked map location using OpenStreetMap Nominatim
  2. Search Bing News RSS for public reports matching the selected place
  3. Sort matching reports chronologically and link to each publisher

EchoMap only searches publicly accessible news metadata. Search coverage depends on the source index and place names; it does not access private accounts or restricted databases. Verify reports with the original publisher.
